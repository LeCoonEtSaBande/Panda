LOL TIER LIST

LEAGUE OF LEGENDS PATCH 16.21

Every patch all League of Legends champions are graded for the LoLalytics LoL Tier List. The highest performing LoL champions are given an S tier while the remaining champions are ranked from A to D tier. LoL Tier List ranks are based upon champion win rate, PBI index, best on champion win rate and best on champion average elo. The best players are determined by their performance over the last 90 days with a minimum rank of Diamond and 50 games played on the champion. Players used for this measurement are listed on the LoLalytics leaderboards.

Champion win rate and win rate delta. The win rate delta is the difference between the win rate using individual player tiers (LoLalytics chosen method) vs the win rate derived from using a game averaged tier (simpler method used by some other websites). LoLalytics data is based upon individual player tiers which includes all games by all players of a given bracket and doesn't include games from players outside the chosen bracket. LoLalytics chosen method provides the most accurate picture of how people of a given tier bracket are performing.
